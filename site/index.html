About Mr.EA

Welcome to Mr.EA¶

Mr.EA is a web site containing reflections, maybe an occassional cautionary tale, and some adviocacy from the authors. It is based upon many years of trans-Atlantic conversations arising from practical and real issues arising from the authors' own experiences in their daily work as Enterprise Architects across many industries.

The authors freely admit that the topics covered are not backed by formal research, which is why the site contents are split into two main streams:

  • Reflections - in which the authors have experienced some form of challenge or a question arising for which there has been no answer recroded within the searching they have subsequently done. These pieces thus, whilst unsupported by any academic practice or research, are written to provide some views of what went through the authors minds
  • Thoughts - in which the authors, in the absence of any formal disciurse, aim to apply some thinking about what might happen, or what might be useful

In all of these pieces, and across the site in general, it is our hope that these arcticles provide some insight and value to anyone entering, or practicing, what is called Entrepirse Architecture or EA. Given the authors names are Murali and Russell, hopefully the site name becomes clear
